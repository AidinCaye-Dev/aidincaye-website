Aidin Caye is a South African DJ and producer from the East Rand of Johannesburg. He started out at 16 as a resident DJ in clubs and bars on the East Rand, too young to be at the parties he was playing, and built a name for unique sets, a feel for the crowd and popular radio mixes. He has since grown from small-town bar sets into one of the country's most respected tastemakers.

He has always sided with music that celebrates artistry over popularity. His roots are in groove-driven tech house, and over the years his sound has found its home in warm, analog-flavoured melodic techno.

His debut single, “Reflections” (2015, Universal Music South Africa), was a collaboration with Liquideep frontman Ziyon. A year later Aidin reworked it with Mark DVB, and the new version charted on 5FM, Good Hope FM and Apple Music and reached the Top 50 of South Africa's overall Shazam chart.

He then spent several years working behind the scenes with record labels and artists, online and in the live industry, and toured the country with DJ and producer TiMO ODV, opening for him at clubs and festivals as the two worked to bring techno to new audiences in South Africa.

In 2019 he went all in on his own brand with “Hypnotize”, his first radio single in over three years. An independent dance release, it peaked in the Top 20 of the South African Shazam chart and passed 300,000 streams. It was followed by his debut EP, *Connections*: eight tracks that show his growth as a producer, with collaborations with long-time friend Apple Gule and singer Kylah Jasmine, club-focused instrumentals, and, for the first time, his own vocals.
