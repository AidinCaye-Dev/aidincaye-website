Aidin Caye is a South African DJ, producer and creative entrepreneur whose music lives in the space between emotion and rhythm. With over 15 years behind the decks, his journey started at 16 with residencies in clubs and bars across Johannesburg's East Rand, and grew into sharing stages with some of the country's most respected acts.

His style blends house and techno with deep electronic grooves and uplifting moments, a balance that has earned him a loyal following and releases on respected labels.

In 2015 he stepped into the spotlight as a producer with his debut single “Reflections”, a collaboration with Ziyon of Liquideep, later reimagined with Mark DVB to success on South African radio and charts. He went on to tour and collaborate with TiMO ODV, refining his sound behind the scenes.

He found his stride as a solo artist with “Hypnotize” in 2019, a melodic house single that broke through independently: without major-label support it passed one million streams and ranked among the Top 20 most Shazamed songs in South Africa. Despite the challenges of 2020 he released his debut EP, *Connections*, and co-produced “We Don't Care” on TiMO ODV's *Midnight Children* EP, a radio and streaming hit.

Off stage, his role in the culture runs deeper. In 2021 he directed and produced *The State of the Arts*, a documentary on the impact of lockdown on South Africa's live entertainment sector, widely covered by national media. That same year he launched The Garden Party, a boutique event series that turns unlikely venues into intentional experiences; it has since grown into one of the country's most anticipated party brands, now running nationally.

Today he champions the next wave of South African talent through his record label, Midnight Children Records, while continuing to push his own sound forward.
